SACRED TRUST

Seva Ethics & Financial Standards

In the Vaishnava tradition, financial stewardship is not mundane bureaucracy—it is a direct practice of devotional integrity. Here are our core operational guidelines.

Sri Sri Radha Shyamsundar
🕉️ Sri Shantipur Dhama Heritage

"Not a Single Paisa Wasted in Krishna's Seva"

Srila Prabhupada instructed that temple funds are sacred Lakshmi offered with great devotion by donors and congregation members. When a devotee handles cash advances for the kitchen, deities, or festivals, they act as a trustee of the Lord.

Srila Prabhupada's Directive:
"Krishna's money must be spent very carefully, not a farthing wasted. You must keep very strict accounts of income and expenditure."
— Letters on Deity Worship & Center Management
Standards

The 4 Golden Pillars for Every Purchaser

1️⃣
Itemized Accuracy

Never log vague labels like "miscellaneous items". Always record exact item descriptions, quantities, and pricing breakdowns for total transparency.

2️⃣
Mandatory Receipts

Always request printed tax bills or signed vendor vouchers from local bazaar merchants. Retain paper bills physically for weekly cashier reconciliation.

3️⃣
Immediate Settlement

Never retain advance change in personal pockets. Settle the balance with the cashbox treasurer immediately upon returning to the ashram or temple.

4️⃣
Devotional Trust

Accounts are not an expression of suspicion; they are an act of protection. Clear accounting preserves cordial relations among devotees and eliminates anxiety.

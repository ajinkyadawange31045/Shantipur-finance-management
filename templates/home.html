The Devotional Dilemma

Why Traditional Accounting Fails Devotional Ashrams

Ashrams don't run like commercial corporations. They run on cash advances for bazaar shopping, fast-moving prasadam kitchens, and volunteer seva.

❌
The "Pocket Change" Leakage

A volunteer takes ₹3,000 for festival decorations, spends ₹2,450, and forgets the remaining ₹550 in their kurta pocket. Without a tracking ledger, weeks pass and Lakshmi is unaccounted for.

🧾
The Missing Voucher Nightmare

Months later when temple audits happen, crumpled receipts are missing or unreadable. Shantipur enforces instant digital tracking with explicit bill presence checks for every entry.

💸
Commercial SaaS Greed

Corporate tools charge ₹1,500 to ₹4,000 per user every month. Ashrams survive on donor charity and cannot bleed sacred seva funds on software licenses. We are 100% donation-sustained.
